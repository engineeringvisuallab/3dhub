Why EVLab

Built like engineering software

Not just shapes. Each model knows its parts, its limits and how it moves.

Rule-checked design

Stair riser and tread rules, valve and flange clearances, safety glass, sash limits. A design that would fail cannot be downloaded.

ON / OFF modes

Doors swing, gates rise, handwheels turn, blinds roll. Every moving part is its own named group and exports as an Open / Close animation.

Real drawings, real DXF

Plan, two sections and four elevations on one A3 sheet with hidden lines removed, hatching, dimensions and section marks. Vector, not a picture.

Groups that make sense

Frame, shutter, hardware, glass, wall. Pick only what you need or take the whole assembly, the same way you would build it on site.

Plugins for your software

One click gives a Blender add-on, a SketchUp extension, AutoCAD LISP and 3D DXF, IFC and a Revit script. Free models stay open.

Free now, premium later

Everything here is free to use with credit to EVLab. Premium (subscription) and a group builder (valve chamber, plant, building) grow from the same data.

How it works

Three steps, no installs

1

Choose a model

Door, window, staircase, valve chamber, pump, boundary wall. Pick the type, design, material and finish.

2

Set the size

Slide the dimensions. The page recalculates, flags problems and shows the drawings live.

3

Download what you need

GLB, OBJ, DXF drawings or a full software package. Only the type you chose is exported.

One model, every software

Open it where you work

Browsers cannot write native .rfa, .skp or .blend files, so each package gives you an add-on, extension or script that builds the model inside your program, with its groups, colours and parameters.

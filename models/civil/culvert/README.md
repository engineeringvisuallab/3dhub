# Culvert under a road embankment

Box, pipe or arch culvert with one to four cells, headwalls, 30 degree wingwalls and aprons, under a road embankment. The length follows the road width, fill and side slope.

- Hydraulics: Manning normal depth in each cell at the bed slope. Checks depth <= 80 % of the height and velocity 0.6 to 4.0 m/s. No inlet control, headwater or tailwater.
- Views: 3D (embankment shown transparent), plan, long section A-A, cross section B-B, typical cell detail, and a **Compare types** table.
- Downloads: GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF 3DFACE, DXF sheet, SVG sheet, spec.json, or ZIP of all. Units are metres.
- Wall and slab thicknesses follow a simple span rule. Structural design, reinforcement, foundation, scour protection and the hydraulic design of the inlet and outlet must be done by an engineer.
- Not included: skewed culverts, culverts under railways, fish passage design, inlet control charts.

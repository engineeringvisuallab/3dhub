EVLab 3D Hub · cells or barrels, headwalls, wingwalls and aprons · hydraulic check (Manning) · 3D, plan, long section, cross section, detail · GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF, SVG

    Indicative planning model. Wall and slab thicknesses follow a simple span rule and are not a structural design: reinforcement, loads (fill, traffic, hydraulic), foundation and scour protection must be designed by an engineer. Hydraulics use Manning normal depth in each cell at the bed slope entered (no inlet control, headwater or tailwater). The 80 % depth and 0.6 to 4.0 m/s limits are typical planning values. Drawings are full size in metres; plot plan and long section 1:100, cross section 1:50, detail 1:25. STEP, IFC and FBX are not produced.

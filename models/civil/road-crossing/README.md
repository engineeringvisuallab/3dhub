# Road Crossing Methods

One carrier pipe under a highway. Pick the method; casing, pits, cover rules, 3D model and drawings follow.

Methods: microtunnelling, pipe jacking (man-entry), auger boring, pipe ramming, box jacking, open cut (sheet-piled trench, pavement reinstatement). HDD is a separate model (`models/civil/hdd-crossing`).

- Views: 3D, plan, long section A-A, cross section at ROW centre, typical detail, and a **Compare methods** table that checks every method against your inputs.
- Cover is measured from the crown of the pipe or casing to the pavement top.
- Downloads: GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF 3DFACE, DXF sheet, SVG sheet, spec.json, or ZIP of all. Same formats and units as the railway model.
- Casing sizes, pit sizes, length limits and cover factors are typical planning values, not a design. Confirm with the contractor, geotechnical report and road authority.

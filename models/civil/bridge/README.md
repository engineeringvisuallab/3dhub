# Road bridge over a river

Simply supported multi-span road bridge: RCC solid slab, RCC T-girder, PSC I-girder or steel plate girder with a composite deck. Abutments and piers stand on pile groups below the scour level.

- Input: spans and span length, carriageway and footpath width, road level, high flood level, bank height, scour depth, pile length and diameter, girder spacing, minimum freeboard.
- Checks: freeboard, span limit for the type, pile embedment below scour, bed width between bank toes, piers on the bed, bearing level above the bank.
- Views: 3D, plan, elevation, cross section at mid-span, cross section at a pier, typical deck and girder detail, and a **Compare types** table.
- Downloads: GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF 3DFACE, DXF sheet, SVG sheet, spec.json, or ZIP of all. Units are metres.
- Girder depths, pier, pile and abutment sizes follow simple rules and are not a structural design. Loads, hydraulics, scour, seismic, bearings, reinforcement and prestress need an engineer and the applicable code.
- Not included: skewed or curved bridges, cable-stayed or arch bridges, railway bridges, expansion joints and drainage details.

Road bridge over a river – slab, T-girder, PSC I-girder, steel girder

EVLab 3D Hub · spans, deck, girders, piers, piles, abutments, HFL and scour · 3D, plan, elevation, cross sections, detail · GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF, SVG

    Indicative planning model for a simply supported multi-span road bridge. Girder depths follow span-to-depth ratios, and pier, pile and abutment sizes follow simple rules. They are not a structural design: loading, hydraulics, scour, seismic, bearings, reinforcement and prestress must be designed by an engineer to the applicable code. Span limits and the pile embedment rule are typical planning values. Drawings are full size in metres; plot plan and elevation 1:200, sections 1:100, detail 1:25. STEP, IFC and FBX are not produced.

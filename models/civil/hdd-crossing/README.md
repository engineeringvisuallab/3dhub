# HDD Highway and Railway Crossing

One HDPE pipe drilled by HDD under a highway first and then a railway, in a single bore.

- Page: `parametric/hdd-crossing.html` (3D view + drawings + downloads).
- Drawn from the parameters: **plan**, **long section A-A** (with chainages and cover), **cross section at highway centre** and **at track centre**. All four go on one sheet.
- Downloads: GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF 3DFACE (3D, Z up, metres), DXF sheet and SVG sheet (drawings), spec.json, or ZIP of all.
- Drawing DXF is R12 ASCII, metres, full size. Plot plan and long section at 1:200, cross sections at 1:50. Layers: A-PIPE, A-CL, A-BORE, A-GRND, A-ROAD, A-RAIL, A-ROW, A-PIT, A-DIM, A-TEXT, A-FRAME.
- GLB / OBJ: metres, Y up. Soil and the reamed bore are view-only and not exported.
- Rules block the download when the bore path does not fit, the crossing is outside the bore length, the bend radius is below 90 x OD (indicative) or cover is below the entered minimum.
- Not included: STEP, IFC, FBX, DWG; geotechnical, pull-back load and frac-out checks; a Studio component (the Studio uses simple box and cylinder parts).
- All figures are indicative. Confirm with the HDD contractor, the geotechnical report and the highway and railway authorities.

HDD Highway and Railway Crossing – parametric

EVLab 3D Hub · one HDPE pipe drilled under a highway and then a railway · 3D model, plan, long section and cross sections · GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF, SVG

    Indicative planning model. Cover, bend radius and pipe data must be confirmed with the HDD contractor, the geotechnical report and the highway and railway authorities. Drawings are drawn full size in metres; plot plan and long section at 1:200, cross sections at 1:50. STEP, IFC and FBX are not produced.

Railway Crossing – trenchless and open-cut methods

EVLab 3D Hub · microtunnelling, pipe jacking, auger boring, pipe ramming, box jacking, open cut · 3D, plan, long section, cross section, detail · GLB, OBJ, STL, DXF, SVG

    Indicative planning model for one carrier pipe under a railway embankment. Casing sizes, pit sizes, limits and cover factors are typical planning values, not a design. Confirm with the contractor, the geotechnical report and the railway authority (cover, monitoring limits, track possession). For HDD use the HDD Highway and Railway Crossing model. Drawings are full size in metres; plot plan and long section 1:200, cross section 1:50, detail 1:20. STEP, IFC and FBX are not produced.

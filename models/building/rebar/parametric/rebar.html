Capacity and rules

      Strength design to ACI 318 (φ = 0.9 tension-controlled, 0.65 tied / 0.75 spiral columns, 0.75 shear, λ = 1). Development, lap and hook lengths use the simplified ACI equations. Short column, axial load only: no slenderness or moment interaction. Confirm with a structural engineer before detailing.

# Reinforcement bar

One page (parametric/rebar.html), three modes.
- Single bar: plain Gr 250, deformed Gr 420, TMT Gr 500, epoxy-coated Gr 420; straight, L, 180° hook, U, cranked, stirrup, spiral, lap. Gives area, kg/m, design tension capacity, ld, ldh, lap and bend rules.
- RC beam cage: bottom and top bars, stirrups. Gives phiMn, phiVn, safe UDL on a span, As,min and spacing checks.
- RC column cage: rectangular tied or circular spiral. Gives phiPn,max, steel ratio and tie rules.
Strength design to ACI 318 (simplified). Short column, axial only. Failing designs cannot be downloaded.

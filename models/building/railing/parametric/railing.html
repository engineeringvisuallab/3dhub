Railing & handrail – posts, infill, gate

Height, post spacing, post section and baluster gap are calculated from the use and the length. Rules are checked live.

    Rules used: handrail height 900 (stairs) / 1000 (balcony) / 1100 (public, high-rise); no gap above 100 mm (95 mm used); bottom gap under 100 mm; cables under 80 mm; line load 0.75 kN/m (1.5 kN/m public) on the handrail; post section from bending stress (steel 150, stainless 120, aluminium 60, wood 8 MPa); grip diameter 32–50 mm on stairs; horizontal rails (climbable) only in public use; glass 12 mm laminated with top rail. Check with a structural engineer.

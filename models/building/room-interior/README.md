# Room Interior (Bedroom)

One page (parametric/room-interior.html): a full bedroom interior. Wardrobe with loft (sliding or hinged doors, hanging, shelves, drawers), bed with headboard wall, bedside cabinets, study desk with chair, bookshelf, dresser with mirror, curtain, rug and false ceiling with cove light.
Room size, window, wardrobe length, bed size and finishes are parameters. Circulation, window area and door swing are checked; failing designs cannot be downloaded.
GLB has Open and Close animations for the wardrobe doors and the room door. Next: drawings/DXF, kitchen cabinet and living room variants.

Staircase – straight, L-shape, U-shape

Riser, tread, landing and slab are calculated from the floor height. Rules are checked live.

    Rules used: riser up to 190 mm, 2 × riser + tread between 550 and 700 mm, tread at least 250 mm, width at least 900 mm, at most 16 risers per flight, handrail 900 mm on flights and 1000 mm on landings, baluster gap under 100 mm, headroom 2100 mm. Slab / stringer depth is span ÷ 22 (RCC) or span ÷ 18 (steel). Structural design must be checked by an engineer.

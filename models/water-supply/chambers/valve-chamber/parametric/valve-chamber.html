Valve Chamber – build your own

Pick a chamber type, add components in flow order. Rules and clearances are checked live.

    Indicative dimensions: EN 558 face-to-face (gate valve), EN 1092-2 flanges, DI pipe OD. Butterfly, PRV/PSV and air valve lengths are typical values, confirm with the manufacturer. SV is modelled as a sluice valve (same size as gate valve). Reducers are concentric.

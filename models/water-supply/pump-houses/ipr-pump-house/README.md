# IPR Pump House (Impounding Reservoir pumping station)

Wet-well pump house built from the IPR pumping station drawing (plan -0.50 m, plan +7.50 m, section A-A, section B-B).

What is modelled: 10 900 x 4 200 pit (400 mm walls and columns, -4.00 m floor, +7.50 m deck with 4 grating openings), river-side wall with 410 x 820 intake holes and steel grids, 4 vertical pumps (2 + 2), 4 steel delivery lines (DN400 default) with the offset bend from the outer pump, one gate valve and one dismantling joint per line, the DN900 header (6400 long, run axis +5.90 m) with its outlet to the Parshall flume, optional concrete basement 6475 x 1225 x 500, and the roofed operator platform above the deck (columns, roof beams, roof slab, MS I-beam monorail over the pumps, optional side walls). Drawing sheet: 2 plans (-0.50 m and deck +7.50 m with roof removed), sections A-A and B-B, 3 elevations.

Rules checked live: delivery and header velocity, bend fit, riser gap, pump gap, deck opening, flange to beam, pipe invert above max WL +5.50 m, cover over header.

Output: GLB (animations Open, Close, Run), OBJ, DXF sheet (2 plans, 2 sections, 3 elevations), PNG, spec.json.

Assumptions to confirm with the project drawings: see "assumptions" in model.json.

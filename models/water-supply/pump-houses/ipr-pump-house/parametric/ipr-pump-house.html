IPR Pump House – Impounding Reservoir pumping station

Wet-well pump house with 4 vertical pumps, 4 × DN400 delivery lines, gate valve + dismantling joint on each line, and the DN900 header to the Parshall flume. Based on the IPR pumping station drawing (plan −0.50 m, plan +7.50 m, section A-A, section B-B) with the roofed operator platform above the deck.

    Sizes follow the drawing: 10 900 × 4 200 pit, 400 mm walls and columns, levels −4.00 / +5.90 / +7.50 m. Flanges (EN 1092-1), steel pipe OD and gate-valve face-to-face (EN 558) are indicative. Pump size, valve make, column base width, the basement position and the intake-hole layout are assumptions: confirm with the project drawings and the pump / valve makers.

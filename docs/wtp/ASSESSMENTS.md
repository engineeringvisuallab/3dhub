# WTP assessments and their calculations

Generated by `node tools/check-wtp.mjs --write`. Each assessment is a separate module with its own calculations.

## Raw water physical assessment

Category: physical. Characterise the physical quality of the source and what it means for treatment.

| Calculation | Inputs | Outputs | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turbidity and suspended solids statistics (mean, percentiles, peaks) (`turbidity-statistics`) | turbidity_series | statistics | planned |
| Seasonal and storm turbidity relation (`turbidity-vs-flow`) | turbidity_series, flow_series | design_peak_turbidity | planned |
| Particle size distribution and settling-column result (`particle-size-settling`) | particle_size_distribution, settling_test | removal_curve | planned |
| Colour and UV254 (`colour-uv254`) | colour, uv254 | organic_matter_indicator | planned |
| Temperature effect on viscosity and settling (`temperature-effect`) | temperature_series | viscosity, design_temperature | planned |
| Conductivity and TDS (`conductivity-tds`) | conductivity | tds | planned |
| Odour and taste screening (`odour-taste-screen`) | threshold_odour_number | control_needed | planned |
| Floating material and debris load (`floating-debris-load`) | debris_survey | screening_class | planned |
| Source quality class from the physical record (`source-physical-class`) | statistics, profile | source_class | planned |

EVLab 3D Studio Build bigger models by attaching ready 3D models

Drag a model into the scene to place it freely. Select an object, then click a model to attach it to the object's free port.

Getting started
  1. Drag a model from the left list into the scene.
  2. Select it, then click another model: it attaches to the free port.
  3. Use Save, Export GLB / OBJ, Drawing (plan, elevations, sections) or BOM (quantities) in the top bar.

Drawing sheet

Bill of materials
